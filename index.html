About Rinix

A small studio, built for founders who move fast.

Rinix is a web development and digital solutions agency for new businesses, startup founders, and teams who need a website or web app that actually works — not just one that looks good in a screenshot.

We keep every build lean: modern, standards-based code with no unnecessary dependencies, so your site loads fast, ranks well, and stays easy to maintain long after launch. Every project is designed and engineered by the same small team from first call to deployment.

How we work

Our process

A clear, sequential path from first conversation to launch day.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We learn your goals, audience, and constraints, then scope the project so there are no surprises later.

  2. 02

    Design

    We design a visual direction and key screens for your review before a single line of production code is written.

  3. 03

    Build

    We develop against the approved design, with regular check-ins so you always know where the project stands.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We test across devices, verify performance and SEO, then deploy to production and confirm everything works live.

  5. 05

    Support

    Post-launch, we're available for maintenance, optimization, and new features as your product grows.
